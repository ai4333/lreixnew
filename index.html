LREIX LAND

Title Risk Scoring for Revenue and Urban Land

  • Bilingual risk explanation

About LREIX

Closing the Gap Between Digitized and Cross-Verified

India has spent over a decade digitizing land records — and digitization alone hasn't made land safer to transact in. A digitized record can still hide a cancelled allotment, an unresolved court case, or a government land overlap.

LREIX closes that gap for the law firms and lenders who carry the legal and financial risk when it goes wrong.
